    Parses the RR PDF's text layer directly — RR No./Date from their fixed-format line, siding from the
    FROM STATION/SIDING code block, company from the SENDER'S NAME field, and wagon table totals summed
    from the per-wagon rows. Wagon count and accepted weight are cross-checked against the PDF's own
    header totals (WGON FRWH / ACTL WGHT) — a mismatch is flagged rather than silently trusted. Requires
    a text-layer PDF (not a scanned image). The siding/company tag is looked up live from your Google
    Sheet's "Siding Reference" tab at save time, not from a copy baked into this app.
